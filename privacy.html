Privacy Policy for PolarExt

Last updated: 2026-11-24

What Data We Collect

  • License Key & Device Identifier: When you choose to activate PolarExt Pro, we store your entered Polar.sh license key, a locally generated random device ID, and activation metadata.
  • Extension Preferences: Your display preferences (appearance theme, preferred export format, highlighter mode, and toast notifications).
  • Active Page SEO Metadata & Links (Local Only): When you ask PolarExt to inspect a tab, page title, meta description, canonical/heading counts, link URLs, and anchor attributes are read locally to compute the audit inside the popup or side panel.

How Data Is Stored

License state, device ID, and the last 10 recent scan snapshots are stored locally in your browser via chrome.storage.local. User preferences (including your selected appearance theme and export format) are stored via chrome.storage.sync so they sync across your signed-in Chrome browsers.

How Data Is Used

  • To compute SEO scores, flag link security issues, and display audit reports locally.
  • To verify Pro license entitlement, device seats, and usage limits via Polar.sh.
  • To remember your theme, highlight, and export preferences.

Third-Party Services

PolarExt connects to Polar.sh (https://api.polar.sh and https://sandbox-api.polar.sh) solely when activating, periodically revalidating, or deactivating a Pro license key. PolarExt does not transmit webpage content, SEO audit results, browsing history, or analytics to any server.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share user data with advertisers or data brokers. Only your license key and anonymous device label are sent to Polar.sh to validate your license.

Data Retention and Deletion

Data remains in your browser storage until you deactivate your license, click Clear All Stored Data in the PolarExt Options page, or uninstall the extension.

Changes to This Policy

We will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page if our data practices change.

Contact

For privacy questions, open an issue on our public support repository or contact the publisher email listed on our Chrome Web Store page.
